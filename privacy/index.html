Pfoliolio Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

Pfoliolio tracks investment holdings across your accounts. It is a tracker, not a brokerage: it executes no trades and gives no financial advice. This policy describes exactly what leaves your device, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

The server

The app has one server, bd-pfolio.fly.dev, operated by the developer on Fly.io. All transport is HTTPS.

Sign-in

When you sign in, the app sends your Apple identity token. We keep Apple's per-app user identifier and, on first sign-in only, the email address Apple supplies. After that the app authenticates with a random session token stored in your device Keychain; the server holds only a SHA-256 hash of it. Sessions expire after 90 days and are revoked server-side when you sign out.

Your investment data

Your holdings travel server-to-server, not from your device. The server retrieves accounts, securities, and holdings from Plaid using a read-only access token.

You enter your brokerage credentials on Plaid's own hosted page in the browser. Neither the app nor our server ever sees your brokerage username or password. Read-only means the connection cannot trade, transfer, or withdraw — that is a property of the Plaid product scope, not a promise we are making on its behalf.

What the app sends

From your device, the server receives what you type: questions to the assistant, manually entered holdings, classification corrections, account nicknames, and allocation targets.

The Q&A assistant — your choice of three

You choose which AI answers your questions, in Settings. Whichever you pick, the payload is the same: your question, up to the last six turns of your conversation, and a JSON snapshot of the same portfolio data the dashboard shows — tickers, security names, dollar values, account names and types, institution names, and the last-four account mask. It never includes your name, email, Apple identifier, or any Plaid token.

  • Anthropic Claude (the default) — the payload goes to Anthropic's servers via our backend; excluded from training under Anthropic's commercial API terms.
  • OpenAI ChatGPT — the payload goes to OpenAI's servers via our backend instead; API content is excluded from training under OpenAI's terms by default.
  • Apple Intelligence — the model runs on your device. For this choice, your question and portfolio snapshot do not leave the device for the Q&A feature at all. (Requires hardware with Apple Intelligence.)

For the two server-side choices, questions and answers are also stored in our database so the conversation has continuity. The app shows each choice's data path right under the picker.

Retention

  • Holdings are replaced on every sync — sold positions are deleted, not archived.
  • Plaid access tokens are kept for as long as an institution stays connected. Disconnecting an institution, or using the in-app reset, deletes the connection and its holdings.
  • Q&A history is kept server-side to provide conversation context.
  • Daily allocation snapshots are kept to draw the history chart.
  • Deleting your account deletes all of your data together, by database-level cascade.

Tracking, advertising, and training

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Nothing is collected for advertising. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared beyond the two service providers named here: Plaid and Anthropic.

Your data is not used to train any model — ours (we have none) or Anthropic's (excluded under their commercial API terms). It is not used for advertising or shared for tracking, in the App Privacy sense or any other.

Deleting your data, in the app

Settings → Delete Account permanently erases your account and everything under it — connections, holdings, classifications, targets, and Ask history — revokes our access at Plaid, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple grant at Apple. Manage Connections → Disconnect does the same for a single institution.
